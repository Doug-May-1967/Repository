Section I

The conical reference frame

A conical reference frame sits inside the classical spherical reference frame common to every orbit type. It shares a common apex and spherical center with that frame and uses classical nomenclature throughout.

Equivalent variable sets

Sphere radii and cone generators are orbit variables, equivalent to the common in-plane variables. Orbital position and motion can be designated using either set, and the sphere-radii/cone-generator set yields a new form of the governing equations.

Families of orbits

Framing orbits on the cone gives a new commonality across orbit families:

  • Elliptical orbits with the same total energy have a unique orientation depending on eccentricity.
  • Hyperbolic orbits are modeled on cones whose apex angle depends on eccentricity.
Elliptical orbits occupy a fixed cone with a 45° half-angle. Hyperbolic orbits occupy a cone with a varying half-angle greater than 45°. \[ \sin(2\Phi_h) = e \quad \text{(elliptical)} \qquad \sin(\Phi_h) = \frac{1}{e} \quad \text{(hyperbolic)} \]
